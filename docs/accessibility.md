# Accessibility controls and verification

The display widget adapts the PartnerSwipe reference components to this site's Next.js
components, fonts, and ink, teal, and gold theme. It provides optional reading preferences;
the site's keyboard access, form labels, focus indicators, and responsive layouts work
independently of the widget.

## Button placement

- Visitors can select **Move button to navigation** in Accessibility options. This hides
  the floating button and saves that choice in their browser. Header, mobile-menu, and
  footer buttons continue to open the options.
- The site owner can choose **Navigation only** under **Site Settings → Accessibility
  Button Placement** in TinaCMS. The source setting is `accessibilityButtonPlacement`
  in `content/site/site-settings.json`: `floating` or `navigation`.
- **Reset display preferences** removes the saved display choices. Choices synchronize
  between tabs, and controls continue to work for the current visit if storage is blocked.

Preferences include 100–200% text, high contrast, dark display, grayscale images,
underlined links, highlighted controls, readable font, line and letter spacing, left
alignment, hidden images, reading guide, and reduced motion. Dark display plus high
contrast uses a black background with white text. Operating-system reduced motion remains
effective after a reset.

## Implementation

- `src/components/ui/Accessibility{Provider,Widget,NavButton}.tsx`: shared controls,
  native modal, keyboard focus handling, and navigation triggers.
- `src/lib/accessibility.ts`: validated, version-independent browser preferences.
- `src/app/layout.tsx`, `globals.css`, and header/footer components: site integration,
  focus visibility, display modes, and measured mobile-navigation clearance.
- Navigation, search, cart, wishlist, and Smart Fit components: dismissible menus and
  safer focus handling.
- Appointment, contact, wedding, About, and PageHero components: accessible labels,
  announced availability, and enlarged-text reflow.
- Home product rails: reduced-motion preferences also control JavaScript scrolling and
  autoplay.
- `tina/config.ts`, site settings types, and `tina/tina-lock.json`: owner placement setting;
  the lock was regenerated by the normal Tina build.
- Privacy policy: disclosure of locally saved accessibility choices.

widget/preference tests passed. The focused preference test exercises all 40,960

or assistive-technology compatibility.

## Remaining review

These checks do not establish full ADA or WCAG conformance. Screen readers, voice control,
Safari/Firefox, external Shopify checkout, live orders, actual email delivery, and
third-party video captions/transcripts were not verified in this change. Content and
third-party integrations need ongoing review against the
[WCAG guidelines](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/) and
[DOJ web accessibility guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/).
